Study Scope

Included Elements

  • Completed Apartment Projects
  • Ongoing Apartment Projects
  • Upcoming Apartment Projects
  • Publicly Available Information

Excluded Elements

  • Non-public project information
  • Confidential company data
  • Undisclosed future developments
  • Internal financial records
Key Assumptions
Assumption 01: Only projects officially published on developer websites were considered for the core dataset.
Assumption 02: Publicly advertised apartment prices represent the actual market pricing conditions at the time of data collection.
Assumption 03: Project information available on official websites was assumed to be accurate and up-to-date.
Assumption 04: The collected project portfolio provides a highly representative overview of each developer's market presence.
Assumption 05: Final transaction prices may slightly differ from initially advertised public prices.
Limitations
Analysis is based solely on publicly available information.
Market conditions and inventory may change after the data collection period.
Advertised market prices may vary from actual final sales transactions.
Unannounced or future project developments are not reflected in current matrices.
