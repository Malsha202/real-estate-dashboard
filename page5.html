Geospatial Analysis of Competitor Apartment Projects

Territorial Mapping & Market Density Assessment

MARKET CONCENTRATION INSIGHTS
  • Highest Project Density: Colombo Core (Colombo 03, 04, and 07) forms the high-density epicenter for luxury developments.
  • Strongest Competition: Periphery borders like Rajagiriya and Nawala show ultra-aggressive multi-developer targeting.
  • Most Developer Activity: Greater Colombo captures 85%+ of active footprint, driven by urban transit infrastructure.
COMPANY DOMINANCE ZONES
  • Colombo: JKP and Fairway hold high-tier luxury territory dominance.
  • Suburbs: Prime Group commands structural dominance in Thalawathugoda, Kottawa, and Malabe.
  • Coastal Regions: Blue Ocean Group and Home Lands Skyline actively build along the Western/Southern shore belts.
  • Emerging Cities: Prime Group confidently leads the pivot into Kurunegala and Kandy hubs.
EMERGING DEVELOPMENT CORRIDORS
  • The Highway Belt: Properties touching expressway interchanges (e.g., Kahathuduwa) demonstrate rapid absorption rates.
  • The Northern Axis: Wattala and Negombo are rapidly converting to vertical living due to airport accessibility.
  • The Tech Segment: The Malabe-Athurugiriya corridor maintains massive momentum for mid-tier lifestyle apartments.
HIGH-COMPETITION HOTSPOTS
  • Rajagiriya (Red Zone): Massive overlap between Fairway, Prime Group, and Global Housing where land premium is peaking.
  • Colombo 06 (Red Zone): Overwhelming saturation of Blue Ocean and Span Tower footprints matching upper-middle income brackets.
  • Nugegoda Junction: A critical central crossroads where nearly all major players collide for regional exposure.
