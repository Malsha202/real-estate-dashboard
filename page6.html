Market Scale & Delivery Dominance

Total Apartment Projects vs. Total Apartment Units Delivered

The Mega-Scale Builders
Companies located high on the Y-Axis execute massive multi-tower structures. They achieve incredibly high unit volumes despite executing a lower overall number of individual projects, maximizing land efficiency. Ex:Jhon Keells Properties, Home Lands Skyline
The Market Penetration Leaders
Companies stretching far across the X-Axis command massive geographical market share. Their strategy relies on building numerous medium-to-high density projects across multiple prime localities. Ex: Prime Group
Strategic Recommendation
For top-tier profitability, Prime Group should leverage its vast market penetration (X-Axis) and pivot towards high-density, vertical mega-projects (Y-Axis push) to maximize unit volume while reducing individual site acquisition and logistical costs.
